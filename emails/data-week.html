ONE MORE THING — WHAT IS BOD?
BOD is "how much oxygen this water would need to digest its own filth." Under 30 is the norm for a drain. Every flowing drain here is at 2–4× that. That's why the river downstream tests zero dissolved oxygen — the bacteria ate it all.
